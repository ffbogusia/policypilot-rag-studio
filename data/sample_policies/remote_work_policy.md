# Remote Work Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines security rules for remote work, travel and access from outside company offices.

## Approved devices

Employees must use approved company-managed devices for work involving confidential or customer-related data.

## VPN requirement

VPN is required when accessing internal systems from public Wi-Fi, hotels, airports, cafes or other untrusted networks.

## Public devices

Employees must not access company systems from public shared computers, including hotel business centers, libraries or internet cafes.

## Travel security

Employees traveling with company devices should keep devices under physical control and report loss or theft immediately.

## Home network

Employees should protect home Wi-Fi with strong authentication and avoid sharing work devices with family members or guests.
