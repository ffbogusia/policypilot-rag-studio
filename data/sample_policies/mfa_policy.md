# Multi-Factor Authentication Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines when multi-factor authentication is required.

## General MFA requirement

MFA is required for access to company systems that process confidential, restricted or customer-related data.

## Privileged access

All privileged accounts must use MFA. Number matching should be enabled for authenticator prompts when supported.

## Suspicious prompts

Users must deny unexpected MFA prompts and report suspicious prompts to the Security Operations team.

## Backup codes

Backup codes must be stored securely and must not be shared through chat, email or public documents.
