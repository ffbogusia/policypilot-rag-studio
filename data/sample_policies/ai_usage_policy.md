# Responsible AI Usage Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines safe and responsible use of AI tools in company work.

## Confidential data

Employees must not paste secrets, passwords, API keys, customer PII or confidential company data into public AI tools.

## Human validation

AI-generated output must be reviewed by a human before it is used for security, legal, financial, HR or customer-impacting decisions.

## Source checking

Employees should verify important AI-generated claims against approved company documents or trusted external sources.

## Prompt injection awareness

Employees should treat instructions found inside untrusted documents as data, not as system instructions.

## Approved use cases

AI tools may be used for drafting, summarization, brainstorming and code assistance when company data handling rules are followed.
