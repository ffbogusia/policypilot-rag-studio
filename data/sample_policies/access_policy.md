# Production Access Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how employees, contractors and interns may access production systems and customer data.

## General access rules

Access to production systems must follow the principle of least privilege. Users should receive only the permissions required for their current business task.

## Contractor access

Contractors may access production systems only when there is a documented business need, manager approval, MFA, and a time-limited access window.

Contractor access must be reviewed at the end of the approved access period.

## Intern access

Interns may not access production systems or customer PII unless explicitly approved by Security Operations and their manager.

## Admin access

Administrative access requires a separate privileged account, MFA, and approval from Security Operations.
