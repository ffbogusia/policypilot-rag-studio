# Privacy and Customer Data Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how employees should handle customer data, personal data and confidential information.

## Data minimization

Employees should access and process only the minimum amount of personal data required for an approved business purpose.

## Customer PII

Customer personally identifiable information must be handled according to data classification rules and access control requirements.

## Data retention

Customer data must not be stored longer than required by approved retention rules.

## Data sharing

Customer data must not be shared with unauthorized users, external parties or public AI tools.

## Human review

Decisions involving sensitive customer data should include appropriate human review and must not rely only on automated output.
